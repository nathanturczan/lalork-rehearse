SATURDAY TECH SUPPORT — LA Laptop Orchestra, Aug 15 2026

Orientation-station manual. Symptom-first: find the sentence the performer just said to you, follow that branch, stop when it works.

CHECK THIS FIRST — ARE THEY IN LA-LAPTOP-ORCHESTRA?

Before anything else, before you look at their screen, ask:

"What ensemble does it say you're in?"

There is one right answer on Saturday: LA Laptop Orchestra.

If they say rehearse-5bwwko, Private Rehearsal, Untitled Ensemble, Demo Ensemble, or any other ensemble name — STOP TROUBLESHOOTING. They are in the wrong room. Everything else you check will look broken, because it is broken, but not for the reason you're about to investigate.

The fix, every time: go back to enter.lalaptoporchestra.com and come in through that page again. Every link on it carries the correct room. In Ableton, use the PERFORMANCE tab → JOIN LA LAPTOP ORCHESTRA.

Where each path shows the ensemble name:

Path Where to look Right answer
Ensemble Jammer top scale badge, right side LA Laptop Orchestra
Ableton (Bridge) banner at the top of the device purple ● PERFORMING — LA LAPTOP ORCHESTRA
Strudel badge at the top of the REPL green ● LIVE — la-laptop-orchestra
Enter (notation / MIDI / portal) line above the scale panel no name at all — see below

Enter is the exception, and it will fool you. Enter hides the ensemble name when you're in the right room, because it would just repeat the LA Laptop Orchestra wordmark already on the page. So in Enter, no ensemble name printed = correct room. If a name is printed above the scale panel, they're somewhere else.

THE ESCALATION RULE

Do not invent fixes. If the symptom is not in this document, say:

"I don't have a verified fix for that. Let's get you to the orientation station."

A wrong fix five minutes before a piece is worse than no fix. Every step below corresponds to something that has actually gone wrong or to behavior the app actually has. Nothing here is "try restarting it and see."

Second rule, from FAILURE_MODES.md: if something breaks, simplify. Don't troubleshoot during the event. A performer who can't connect can listen, share a device, or play acoustically. The piece is not precious.

THE SYMPTOM TREE

1. PHONE / ENSEMBLE JAMMER — "I can't hear anything"

Ensemble first (top of this document). Then, in this order:

1a. Are they on an instrument that hasn't been started?

This is the most likely cause and it looks exactly like a broken app.

The JAM link assigns an instrument at random out of seven. Three of those seven make no sound at all until you press a play button:

Instrument Starts silent?
Melody Maker YES — press ▶
Drone YES — press ▶
SNaPS YES — press ▶
Harp no — touch the strings
X/Y Lead no — drag on the pad
Venn no — tap a region
Looper no — tap a pad

The control is a ▶ button in the bar with the volume slider; it turns into ■ once it's running. On a phone it's compact and easy to miss.

So: if they have Melody Maker, Drone, or SNaPS — have them press ▶. Roughly two in five people who come in through JAM will land on one of these.

If they'd rather have something that makes noise the moment you touch it, switch them to Harp in the instrument picker.

1b. Is their own volume down?

There is a mixer with a volume slider per instrument plus a master labeled Main (headphones icon on a phone). A slider at 0% is silent and looks like a dead app. Check their instrument's slider and Main.

1c. Are they actually doing the interaction that instrument wants?

Each one needs a specific gesture, and "I tapped it" isn't always enough:

Instrument What makes sound
Harp swipe/tap across the strings (swipe speed = how loud)
X/Y Lead drag on the pad — a single tap barely does anything
Venn tap inside a circle region
Looper tap a pad
Melody Maker press ▶, then tap grid cells to fill in notes
Drone press ▶
SNaPS press ▶
Tilt / Compass hold the pad at the bottom and tilt/turn — silent if you're not holding the pad

1d. iPhone: silent switch and media volume

  1. Silent/ring switch on the left edge → flip it so no orange shows.
  2. Volume up, pressed while the app is making sound. iOS keeps ringer and media volume separate, so pressing volume on the home screen can move the wrong one.
  3. Bluetooth — audio may be going to headphones, a car, or a speaker in another room.

(not device-tested) The app is written to ignore the silent switch — the native iOS app and the browser on iOS 16.4+ both set the audio session to "playback". So in theory the switch shouldn't matter, and on a modern iPhone this step may do nothing. It costs three seconds and older iOS does still obey the switch, so check it — but if it doesn't help, go to 1a, don't keep pushing on the phone's volume.

1e. Have they touched the screen yet?

Browsers refuse to play audio until the user touches the page. In Jammer the first touch on the instrument is that permission gesture, so this usually resolves itself — but if they've been staring at a silent screen without touching it, have them tap a key.

1f. Did it go silent after locking the phone or switching apps?

iOS suspends audio in the background. Return to Jammer and tap the screen once. Tell them to keep the phone awake.

Still silent after all of that → orientation station. Don't improvise.

2. PHONE / ENSEMBLE JAMMER — "It makes sound but the harmony isn't changing"

This is a different problem from no sound. Don't run the volume checks.

  1. Ensemble. Top scale badge, right side. Must read LA Laptop Orchestra. If it doesn't, that's the whole answer — back through enter.lalaptoporchestra.com → JAM.
  2. Is the conductor actually moving? Look at the TV or another performer's screen. If nobody's harmony is changing, it's not their phone.
  3. Refresh and re-enter through the event path — enter.lalaptoporchestra.com → JAM. Do not have them navigate inside the app or pick a room from a lobby.

Jammer has no persistent connection indicator while you're playing — there is no "connected" light to check. The ensemble name in the badge is the only tell. If the room genuinely failed to load they'd have seen Connecting to room…, Room not found., or Error: … instead of the instrument.

3. ABLETON — "I'm not connected / the harmony isn't changing"

Ensemble first: the banner must be purple ● PERFORMING — LA LAPTOP ORCHESTRA.

The #1 question of the day: a gray device is not broken

ENTER YOUR PRIVATE REHEARSAL CODE in gray means idle, not error.

The Bridge does not auto-connect and never remembers a room. Room codes do not save with a Live Set. Every single time Live opens, every performer must join again.

Fix: PERFORMANCE tab → JOIN LA LAPTOP ORCHESTRA.

Joining is global across the Set. Pressing JOIN on one Bridge joins every Bridge in that Live Set — you don't need to do it per track. The REHEARSAL/PERFORMANCE tab is synced across all instances too.

Red ✗ CHECK ROOM + WIFI

In order: 1. Are they on the venue Wi-Fi? Most common cause. 2. Are they on the PERFORMANCE tab? A typo'd code on the REHEARSAL tab gives red. Switch to PERFORMANCE and press JOIN. 3. Press JOIN LA LAPTOP ORCHESTRA again — it retries. 4. Still red → orientation station. Stop digging.

Amber connecting... for more than ~10 seconds: treat as red, above.

Green ● REHEARSING — PRIVATE ROOM during the show

They're in their practice room. PERFORMANCE tab → JOIN.

Banner is right but the Scale/Chord fields are frozen

They've lost the room. Rejoin: PERFORMANCE → JOIN.

They're typing a room code

On Saturday nobody types a code — that's the REHEARSAL tab. Send them to PERFORMANCE. (For reference: the field accepts room ID, slug, or friendly name, case-insensitive, spaces OK.)

4. ABLETON — "It's connected but I get no sound"

Banner purple or green means the Bridge is fine. This is an audio problem, not a LALORK problem.

  1. Is the track armed / monitor on?
  2. Is the track muted, or the master down?
  3. Is Live's audio output the right device (headphones vs interface)?
  4. Are they actually playing notes? The Bridge is silent until it receives MIDI. It re-pitches what you play; it does not generate notes on its own.

5. STRUDEL — "I'm not connected / the harmony isn't changing"

Ensemble first: green ● LIVE — la-laptop-orchestra.

The room name is baked into the pre-loaded jam — nobody types it — so a wrong room here means they didn't come in through enter.lalaptoporchestra.com → CODE → Open Strudel (jam pre-loaded). Send them back through those three clicks, then Ctrl+Enter to play.

Symptom Fix
Amber ● REHEARSAL — rehearse-xxxxxx Practice room, not the show. Re-open the jam from Enter → CODE.
Red waiting for room "…" Room name typo, or the room isn't open yet. It connects by itself once the room exists.
Green badge, no sound Browser blocked audio. Click the page, press play again.
Plays but never changes The room isn't broadcasting — check with the conductor, not the performer.
Badge invisible in dark mode Known bug. Dark themes (seen in Brave). The badge is there, just unreadable. Not a connection problem — don't chase it.

Don't turn this into API documentation. If they want more than the starter jam, that's a conversation for after the show.

6. ENTER NOTATION (READ) — "My score isn't updating / looks wrong"

Ensemble first — and remember Enter's inversion: no ensemble name printed above the scale panel = correct room. A name printed = wrong room.

READ is completely passive. There is nothing to join, no button to press. It follows the room by itself.

What they see Meaning
Connecting to the ensemble… Still loading. Wait, then refresh.
Waiting for scale data from the ensemble… Room loaded, conductor hasn't broadcast yet. Not an error.
Room "…" not found. Wrong URL. Go to enter.lalaptoporchestra.com.

Known bug: wrong sharps/flats on the incoming chord

The accidentals on the preview / next chord are frequently wrong (it may show C-E-G when the next chord is C♯ major). This is a known bug, not their screen.

Workaround: use the Triads view — it is correct.

Related: the spelling shown is sometimes not the ideal one (sharps where flats would read better). Also known. Not worth troubleshooting on Saturday.

The Rehearse app is not for performers

At the bottom of the Enter portal there is an Open Rehearse → link. On Saturday nobody but Nathan follows it. Rehearse is the conductor app — Nathan drives the entire performance from it. A performer who starts a rehearsal there creates a private rehearse-… room and pulls themselves out of the show.

If someone has ended up in Rehearse: close that tab, go back to enter.lalaptoporchestra.com. No other diagnosis needed.

7. MIDI PATH — no ports, or nothing coming through

Mac: Audio MIDI Setup → Show MIDI Studio → double-click IAC Driver → check "Device is online" → add a port.

Windows: install and run loopMIDI (tobias-erichsen.de/software/loopmidi.html) → create a port.

Then in Enter's MIDI tab: pick the Port, set Channel (1–16), set Octave (±12 semitones per click), and choose Output:

Output Sends
Scale Pitch Classes Every note of the scale, in one octave. Usually wants transposing up.
Scale Root Root of the scale
Chord Root Root of the chord
Chord Pitch Classes Every note of the chord, in one octave. Usually wants transposing up.
Chord Original Voicing Jazz comping voicing. Some voicings are rootless, so the bass note may not be the chord root — expected, not a bug.

No ports in the dropdown: the virtual port wasn't created, or their DAW grabbed it exclusively. Redo the IAC/loopMIDI step, then refresh the Enter page. Also: the browser must be allowed MIDI access — if that prompt was dismissed, refresh and accept it.

STILL BROKEN? → ORIENTATION STATION

Say the words: "Let's get you to the orientation station." That is a complete, correct answer. These in particular have no verified self-serve fix — don't guess:

  • An iPhone still silent after §1a–1f. The code already ignores the silent switch, so a phone that stays quiet is doing something undocumented.
  • Monitoring / performers can't hear themselves. Known past failure, no documented fix.
  • Audio interface / driver problems (incl. MOTU + Windows).
  • Windows-specific Ableton issues. Almost all testing was on Mac.
  • Which sounds/presets to use in the template. Not documented.
  • Conductor cues disappearing. Known open bug, conductor-side. Nothing a performer can do.
  • Anything about the Rehearse app. It's Nathan's, and he's conducting.
  • Anything involving someone's personal Live Set, plugins, or licensing.

And if it's bigger than one person's laptop:

If Then
Wi-Fi dies Local hotspot
TV dies Gather around a laptop
Projector dead Ignore it
Someone can't connect They listen, or share a device
Browser bug Refresh, or skip that station
Too loud Turn down, or go acoustic
A piece falls apart Stop, reset, try again

Ultimate fallback: the Oliveros meditation works with zero technology.

What "working" sounds like

They play notes; the notes come out inside the current harmony. When the conductor changes chord, held notes re-pitch. Rhythm, timbre, and when to play stay entirely theirs.

Known gotchas

  1. The URLs printed inside the Bridge device are wrong. The device shows rehearse.laptoporchestra.com and enter.laptoporchestra.com — missing the second "la". Those domains don't resolve. The real ones are rehearse.lalaptoporchestra.com and enter.lalaptoporchestra.com. If someone says "the site is down," check what they typed. (Fixing this needs a device refreeze — after Saturday, not before.)

  2. The Bridge never remembers a room. Every Live restart = rejoin.

  3. Old 207 KB Bridge still in circulation. Correct file is 212,811 bytes.

  4. Enter hides the ensemble name when you're in the right room. No name = correct.

  5. Strudel badge is invisible in dark mode. Cosmetic only.

  6. There is no Android app. No Play Store listing exists. Android goes through the browser (Chrome), which is the recommended path anyway — don't send anyone hunting for an app that isn't there. No Android-specific audio failures are documented; if one appears, treat it as undocumented and escalate.

  7. The iOS app does not join the show by itself. There is an iPhone app on the App Store, and Enter offers it. It opens into sign-in → ensemble lobby, where they must find LA Laptop Orchestra themselves. This is where "I downloaded the app and I'm in the wrong place" comes from. The Enter → JAM link is the path that joins automatically — recommend that instead. Private rehearse-… rooms are hidden from the lobby and LA Laptop Orchestra is pinned to the top, but other people's public ensembles are listed and tappable by mistake, so confirm the badge afterwards.
